Partition Function when system \(A\) exchanges energy and momentum with the reservoir

Let there be energy and momentum exchange between system \(A\) and reservoir \(A^{'}\)
Total Energy
✦
\(\epsilon\) \(=\) \(E\) \(+\) \(P^2\) \(2M\)
where
\(P\) , momentum of center of mass
\(M\) , center of mass

Conservation Laws

when system is in \(r^{\text{th}}\) state.
Energy (total) \[ \epsilon_r + \epsilon^{'} = \epsilon^{*} \] momentum \[ P_r + P^{'} = P^{*} \] \[ P_r \propto \Omega^{'}(E^{'}) \] \[ P_r \propto e^{-\beta(\epsilon_r - V^{*}\cdot P_r)} \]
where
\(\epsilon_r\) , total enegry of the system \(S\) in \(r^{\text{th}}\) state
\(V^{*}\) , velocity of the center of mass of the combined system
\(P_r\) , momentum of the system \(A\) in \(r^{\text{th}}\) state
\[ \mathrm{Z} = \displaystyle\sum_r e^{-\beta(\epsilon_r - V^{*} \cdot P_r)} \]
In general, when \(E_n\), \(N\) and \(P\) are exchanged, partition function is
\[ P_r \propto e^{-\beta (\epsilon_r - v^{*}P_r - \mu N_r)} \] \[ P_r = \frac{e^{-\beta (\epsilon_r - v^{*}P_r - \mu N_r)}}{\displaystyle\sum_r e^{-\beta (\epsilon_r - v^{*}P_r - \mu N_r)}} \]
✦ Problem 1 probability with velocity distribution of the molecule
Problem
Consider a molecule in a ideal gas system \(A\), which is in contact with reservoir \(A^{'}\) that is moving with constant velocity \(V_0\). The system can exchange energy and momentum with the reservoir. Starting from the equilibrium probability distribution \[ \large P_r \propto e^{-\beta(\epsilon_r - V_0 \cdot P_r)} \] Show that probability with velocity distribution of the molecule is given by \[ \large P(v) \propto e^{-\frac{1}{2}\beta m (v - v_0)^2} \]
Solution
\[ \epsilon_r = \frac{1}{2}mv_r^2 \] \[ P_r = mv_r \] \[ \large P_r \propto e^{-\beta \left(\frac{1}{2}mv_r^2 - mv_0 v_r\right)} \] \[ \large P_r \propto e^{-\beta\left(\frac{1}{2}mv_r^2 - 2 \left(\frac{1}{2}m_r\right)v_0 + \frac{1}{2}mv_0^2 - \frac{1}{2} mv_0^2\right)} \] \[ P \propto e^{-\frac{1}{2} \beta m \left(v_r - v_0 \right)} \cdot e^{\beta \frac{1}{2} m v_0^2} \] \[ P(v_r) \propto \text{ constant } \cdot e^{-\frac{1}{2} m \beta (v_r - v_0)^2} \] \[ P(v_r) \propto \text{ constant } \cdot e^{-\beta \left[ \frac{1}{2} m (v_r - v_0)^2 \right]} \] \[ P(V_r) \propto e^{-\beta E_r} \]

This is Maxwell-Boltzman velocity distribution function
