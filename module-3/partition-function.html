Partition Function when system \(A\) exchanges energy and momentum with the reservoir

Let there be energy and momentum exchange between system \(A\) and reservoir \(A^{'}\)
Total Energy \[ \epsilon = E + \frac{P^2}{2M} \]
where
\(P\) , momentum of center of mass
\(M\) , center of mass

Conservation Laws

when system is in \(r^{\text{th}}\) state.
Energy (total) \[ \epsilon_r + \epsilon^{'} = \epsilon^{*} \] momentum \[ P_r + P^{'} = P^{*} \] \[ P_r \propto \Omega^{'}(E^{'}) \] \[ P_r \propto e^{-\beta(\epsilon_r - V^{*}\cdot P_r)} \]
where
\(\epsilon_r\) , total enegry of the system \(S\) in \(r^{\text{th}}\) state
\(V^{*}\) , velocity of the center of mass of the combined system
\(P_r\) , momentum of the system \(A\) in \(r^{\text{th}}\) state
\[ \mathrm{Z} = \displaystyle\sum_r e^{-\beta(\epsilon_r - V^{*} \cdot P_r)} \]
In general, when \(E_n\), \(N\) and \(P\) are exchanged, partition function is
\[ P_r \propto e^{-\beta (\epsilon_r - v^{*}P_r - \mu N_r)} \] \[ P_r = \frac{e^{-\beta (\epsilon_r - v^{*}P_r - \mu N_r)}}{\displaystyle\sum_r e^{-\beta (\epsilon_r - v^{*}P_r - \mu N_r)}} \]
